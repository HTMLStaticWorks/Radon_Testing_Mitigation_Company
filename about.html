01 • OUR BACKGROUND & ORIGIN

Built on Empirical Physics, Not Guesswork or Fear

Radenza was established in 2011 by licensed environmental engineer Dr. Marcus Vance to replace crude industry guesswork with building physics. Traditional installers mounted generic fans to arbitrary basement corners—causing noisy motor vibration, energy waste, and radon levels that barely complied with federal limits.

“Radon mitigation is not rough plumbing. It is a sub-surface fluid mechanics challenge. When you measure pressure differentials down to a thousandth of an inch of water column, you know the air is safe.”
— Dr. Marcus Vance, PE, C-NRPP, Founder & Chief Building Scientist

By establishing Pressure Field Extension (PFE) diagnostics as our baseline, Radenza has permanently protected over 10,000 single-family, school, and commercial buildings—delivering a verified average reading of 0.78 pCi/L, well below the federal 4.0 action threshold.

04 • TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES

The Four Pillars of Radenza Engineering

Unlike general contractors who treat radon as generic pipe fitting, Radenza approaches every building as a dynamic aerodynamic system.

PILLAR 01 • SOIL MECHANICS

Sub-Slab Pressure Field Extension (PFE)

We drill precise diagnostic micro-test holes in the slab furthest from the proposed suction pit. Using calibrated digital micromanometers, we test how negative vacuum propagates across the aggregate base layer. This guarantees your fan draws radon evenly across every square foot of foundation.

Standard Target: -0.015 in. W.C. Minimum Pressure Drop Across Perimeter
PILLAR 02 • SENSOR TELEMETRY

Continuous Radon Monitoring (CRM)

Instead of cheap passive charcoal pouches susceptible to humidity distortion, Radenza deploys lab-calibrated dual-chamber pulsed ionization detectors. We record hourly radon spikes, barometric dips, indoor temperature, and tamper sensor logs for irrefutable clarity.

EPA & AARST Device Protocol: Dual-Detector Chamber Verification
PILLAR 03 • ARCHITECTURAL INTEGRATION

Aesthetic Routing & Acoustic Decoupling

We take immense pride in your home's appearance. We route exhaust pipes through interior chases, garages, or closet chases whenever feasible. All piping is heavy Schedule 40 PVC, with flexible elastomeric anti-vibration couplings that reduce motor resonance to whispers below 32 dBA.

Acoustic Isolation: <32 dBA Whisper-Quiet Operation Inside Living Spaces
PILLAR 04 • COMPLEX FOOTPRINTS

Commercial & Multi-Family Systems

Large footprint structures present HVAC makeup air balance, high volume slab penetrations, and regulatory hurdles (HUD, OSHA, school safety protocols). Radenza designs multi-pit vacuum manifolds that balance draw without straining building pressurization.

Compliant with ANSI/AARST RMS-MF-2023 & HUD Multifamily Standards
