What Affects Radon Project Scope?

Every property presents a unique structural footprint. The following engineering variables influence project complexity.

PROPERTY SIZE & FOOTPRINT Primary Footprint Variable

Larger foundation slabs or sprawling single-story layouts may require multiple suction extraction points.

FOUNDATION TYPE & SUB-SLAB DENSITY Structural Variable

PRICING CLARIFICATION

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Find quick answers regarding radon service pricing estimates, foundation scope considerations, and system maintenance.

What factors affect the overall cost of a radon mitigation system?
Radon mitigation pricing depends on property square footage, foundation type (full basement, slab-on-grade, or crawlspace), sub-slab soil permeability, suction pit location, PVC vent pipe routing complexity, and inline fan airflow requirements.
Is an initial radon test included in the mitigation pricing estimate?
Initial continuous radon monitoring (CRM) or analytical test placement is quoted separately to baseline indoor radon levels. However, post-mitigation verification testing can be bundled into full active depressurization system installation packages.
How long does a typical radon mitigation system installation take?
Most residential active sub-slab depressurization installations are completed in 3 to 5 hours by certified technicians, including diagnostic pressure field testing, foundation sealing, PVC pipe installation, fan mounting, and system calibration.
Are there recurring operating or maintenance costs after installation?
Active mitigation systems run continuously on low-wattage silent EC inline fans with minimal electricity usage ($3 to $7/month). We recommend replacing inline fan units every 5–8 years and conducting routine verification re-testing every 2 years.
