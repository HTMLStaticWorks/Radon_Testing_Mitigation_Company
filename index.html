ENGINEERED PROTECTION

Radon Mitigation Solutions

We design and install custom Active Soil Depressurization (SSD) systems engineered to reduce indoor radon levels below EPA guidelines.

SUB-SLAB

Active Sub-Slab Depressurization (SSD)

Creates a continuous negative pressure vacuum below the concrete slab to draw soil gas into vent pipes before entering living spaces.

CRAWLSPACE

Sub-Membrane Encapsulation & Venting

Heavy-duty 12-mil poly vapor barrier installation combined with active depressurization piping to seal and exhaust crawlspace soil gas.

DRAIN-TILE

Sump Pit & Perimeter Drain Depressurization

Seals existing sump basins with airtight polycarbonate covers and connects active inline suction fans directly to sub-slab drainage networks.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

How It Works: Our 5-Step Process

A transparent, scientific workflow ensuring complete peace of mind from initial inspection to post-mitigation re-testing.

STEP 01

Initial Property Inspection

Our certified technician inspects foundation type, sump pits, utility penetrations, and ventilation paths.

STEP 02

Deploy Continuous Test Monitor

Place calibrated continuous monitors in lowest lived-in areas to document precise hourly radon concentrations.

STEP 03

System Design & Estimate

If mitigation is needed, we design a custom sub-slab active depressurization plan with fixed, transparent pricing.

STEP 04

Precision System Installation

Install schedule-40 PVC suction piping, whisper-quiet EC fan, airtight sump cover, and real-time U-tube manometer gauge.

STEP 05

Verification & Post-Test Report

Conduct follow-up testing to verify system performance and issue official certification confirming safe indoor air levels.
