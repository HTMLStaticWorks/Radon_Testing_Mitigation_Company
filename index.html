BUILDING SCIENCE & SOIL GAS

Radon Is Invisible. Testing Makes It Measurable.

Radon is a naturally occurring gas formed by the breakdown of trace minerals in rock and soil beneath buildings. Because it is odorless and invisible, standard indoor air quality testing provides clear property data.

INVISIBLE

Cannot Be Detected by Senses

Radon gas has no odor, color, or taste, requiring calibrated continuous monitors or analytical detectors.

VARIABLE

Differs Between Properties

Building foundation design, soil permeability, and ventilation rates create unique indoor conditions for every structure.

TESTABLE

Precise Measurement Available

Standard testing protocols provide accurate data on property-specific radon concentrations over defined timeframes.

EVIDENCE-BASED SAFETY

You Can't Judge Radon Levels by Looking at a Building.

Two identical adjacent homes can have vastly different radon concentrations due to subsurface geology and sub-slab pressure dynamics.

01

No Assumptions

A building's age, luxury architectural finish, or neighborhood location does not indicate its subsurface soil gas migration rate.

02

Measure

Deploying calibrated CRM continuous monitors or passive canisters documents measured indoor radon concentration accurately.

03

Decide

With verified test data in hand, property owners can evaluate whether active mitigation or routine follow-up re-testing is appropriate.

STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE

From First Test to Verified Mitigation

Our structured technical workflow ensures every property assessment follows clear, scientific inspection steps.

STEP 01

Assess Property & Scope

Review building foundation type (basement, slab, crawlspace), ventilation systems, and specific testing guidelines.

STEP 02

Deploy Measurement Equipment

Place calibrated continuous radon monitors (CRM) or passive devices in lowest lived-in areas according to standard testing placement rules.

STEP 03

Review & Interpret Results

Retrieve test data and generate a clear, factual report outlining measured concentrations in context with standard guidance.

STEP 04

Mitigate (If Appropriate)

Design and install a sub-slab active depressurization (SSD) system tailored to building architecture where reduced levels are desired.

STEP 05

Post-Mitigation Re-Test

Conduct follow-up verification testing to confirm mitigation system performance and operational effectiveness.
