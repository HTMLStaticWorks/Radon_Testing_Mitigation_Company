SYSTEM ENGINEERING EXPLAINER

How a Radon Mitigation System Works

An active sub-slab depressurization (SSD) system creates continuous negative pressure beneath the building foundation to gather soil gas before it enters your indoor living space and routes it harmlessly outside.

01

Suction Collection Point

A suction pit excavated beneath the slab gathers soil gas from under the building foundation.

02

Sealed PVC Vent Piping

Sealed Schedule-40 PVC piping provides a dedicated low-resistance pathway through the building structure.

03

Inline Mitigation Fan

A continuously operating inline fan maintains uninterrupted negative pressure beneath the slab.

04

Outdoor Roof Exhaust

Vents soil gas safely above the roofline where atmospheric dispersion naturally dilutes it.
