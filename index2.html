AIR DYNAMICS

How Sub-Slab Pressure Affects Indoor Air

The stack effect, exhaust ventilation, and barometric fluctuations draw soil gas upward through foundation micro-cracks.

Stack Effect Pressure

Warm indoor air rising creates negative pressure at lower building levels, pulling soil gas into basements and crawlspaces.

Mechanical Exhaust

HVAC air handlers, kitchen hoods, and bathroom fans exhaust indoor air, amplifying sub-slab pressure differentials.

Permeable Pathways

Unsealed concrete joints, utility line penetrations, and sump basins provide zero-resistance routes for soil gas entry.

Sub-Slab Active Depressurization (SSD) Architecture

Active mitigation works by reversing natural sub-slab soil pressure, creating negative vacuum pressure below the foundation to draw soil gas safely into sealed suction piping and vent it above the roofline.
